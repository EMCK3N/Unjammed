# Unjammed 🚂

A calm train yard puzzle for kids and grown-ups.

Trains are jammed in the yard, and each one can only drive the way its locomotive points.
Free a train and it rolls up to a platform. Passengers board front to back, and only onto
a train in their own colour. When a train is full, it steams off.

- 100 levels that get harder step by step
- Every colour has its own shape, so you can play colour blind
- No ads, no timers, no tracking
- Works offline and can be added to the home screen on iPhone and iPad
